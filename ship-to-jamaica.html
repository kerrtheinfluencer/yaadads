How Jamaica car import costs work

Customs charges start from the CIF value: car price + shipping + insurance. Import duty is added first, then Special Consumption Tax (SCT), then 15% GCT is charged on the running total. An environmental levy, a customs admin fee, broker fees and port charges come on top. Import licence rules and age limits apply, so check with the Trade Board before you buy.

How is the cost of importing a car to Jamaica calculated?

How old can an imported car be?

Published guidance says standard cars should generally be 6 years old or newer, and an import licence is needed before shipping. Confirm the current rule with the Trade Board.
